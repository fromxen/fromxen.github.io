02 / A little about me

A little about me.

Hey, I'm Xen.

I'm a Personal Support Worker based in Northern Ontario. Outside of work, I'm usually fishing, cooking, gaming, or tinkering with random things.

I've always liked taking photos with my phone, but recently started getting into photography with an actual camera. Still figuring things out, but it's been fun learning something new.

I made FromXen mostly to have a place for the photos and memories I've collected along the way. Figured it'd be nice to have them all in one spot instead of leaving everything in my camera roll.

That's pretty much it. Just sharing a few things I've captured here and there.
